# Product Line Pricing

## Pricing Philosophy

The startup should price based on business value, not only AI minutes.

AI costs matter internally, but customers should understand packages in terms of:

- Kiosks.
- Locations.
- Videos.
- Courses.
- Languages.
- Integrations.
- Support level.

## AI Digital Receptionist

Suggested pricing:

| Package | Target | Setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Pilot | Event, small office, demo location | USD 1,500-5,000 | USD 300-800 |
| Business | Mall, corporate reception, university | USD 5,000-15,000 | USD 800-2,000 |
| Enterprise | Bank, telecom, government, large company | Custom | USD 20,000+/year |
| Premium Live Enterprise | Sonatrach/Sonelgaz-style high-profile deployments | Custom | Custom usage-based or committed contract |

Drivers:

- Number of kiosks.
- Number of locations.
- Languages.
- Integrations.
- Custom avatar/voice.
- Support and SLA.
- Avatar policy: full live premium, budgeted premium, adaptive hybrid, or lite first.

Avatar policy should be a pricing lever:

- Full Live Premium: highest price, best experience, usage-based or committed spend.
- Budgeted Premium: premium experience with hourly/daily/monthly spend controls.
- Adaptive Hybrid: cached premium answers plus lightweight live answers.
- Lite First: lowest cost, strict controls, premium only for selected approved answers.

## AI Presenter Studio

Suggested pricing:

| Package | Target | Monthly |
|---|---|---:|
| Internal/Beta | AlgeriaTech and pilot creators | Free/internal |
| Creator | Small media team | USD 49-199 |
| Business | Company/agency content team | USD 300-1,000 |
| Enterprise | Multi-team content operations | Custom |

Drivers:

- Number of videos.
- Render minutes.
- Brand kits.
- Team seats.
- Custom avatar/voice.
- YouTube/social integrations.

## AI Learning

Suggested pricing:

| Package | Target | Monthly/Annual |
|---|---|---:|
| Starter | Small training team | USD 100-300/month |
| Business | HR/training department | USD 500-2,000/month |
| Enterprise | Large company/institution | Custom annual |

Drivers:

- Number of courses.
- Number of learners.
- Video generation volume.
- SCORM/xAPI/LMS export.
- Compliance review.
- Analytics and certificates.

## Cost Control Across Products

- Cache reusable videos.
- Reuse intros, outros, templates, avatars, and voices.
- Use cheaper text/audio responses for low-value live interactions.
- Batch video generation in background.
- Set customer-level usage limits.
- Track cost per conversation, cost per video, and cost per course.
