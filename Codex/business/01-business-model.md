# Business Model

## Pricing Strategy

Sell as a managed B2B product, not as a cheap self-serve app.

Customers are paying for:

- Better visitor experience.
- Fewer repeated staff questions.
- Multilingual support.
- Modern brand image.
- Analytics.
- Setup and support.

## Package Ideas

### Pilot Package

Target:

- Small company.
- Event booth.
- Coworking space.
- Demo location.

Includes:

- 1 kiosk setup.
- Limited answer library.
- Arabic/French/English.
- Basic analytics.

Possible pricing:

- Setup: USD 1,500 to 5,000.
- Monthly: USD 300 to 800.

### Business Package

Target:

- Mall.
- Corporate reception.
- University.
- Private clinic.

Includes:

- 1-3 kiosks.
- Admin dashboard.
- Approved answer workflow.
- QR codes.
- Human handoff.
- Monthly optimization.

Possible pricing:

- Setup: USD 5,000 to 15,000.
- Monthly: USD 800 to 2,000.

### Enterprise/Government Package

Target:

- Bank.
- Telecom.
- Government office.
- Large enterprise.

Includes:

- Integrations.
- SLA.
- Custom security.
- Private deployment options.
- Advanced analytics.
- Dedicated support.

Possible pricing:

- Custom annual contract.
- USD 20,000+ per year depending scope.

## Revenue Streams

- Setup fee.
- Monthly software subscription.
- Hardware rental or resale.
- Custom avatar creation.
- Custom voice creation.
- Language packs.
- Integration fees.
- Maintenance contract.
- On-premise/private deployment fee.
- Additional kiosk licenses.

## Cost Control

Primary cost risks:

- Live avatar streaming.
- Speech-to-text usage.
- LLM calls.
- Video generation.
- Storage and bandwidth.

Cost controls:

- Cached approved answer videos.
- Idle mode instead of always-on avatar.
- Confidence thresholds.
- Cheaper TTS/audio for live unknown questions.
- Batch video generation.
- Customer-specific usage limits.
