# Go To Market Algeria

## First Customer Profile

The best first customer should have:

- Many repeated visitor questions.
- A visible reception or information desk.
- Interest in technology and innovation.
- Low regulatory risk.
- Ability to approve a pilot quickly.

Best early targets:

- Events.
- Malls.
- Coworking spaces.
- Private schools.
- Corporate headquarters.
- Clinics with non-medical information desks.

## Avoid As First Customer

- Government ministries.
- Banks.
- Hospitals for medical guidance.
- Any customer requiring full procurement before a demo.

These can become valuable later, but they are slow and risky as the first validation step.

## Sales Story

Simple pitch:

> We install a multilingual AI digital receptionist that answers repeated visitor questions, guides people, shows QR codes, and learns from new questions while keeping approved answers under your control.

## Demo Strategy

Build a portable demo:

- Laptop or large tablet.
- External microphone.
- Speaker.
- Big screen if available.
- Preloaded customer-like scenario.

Demo questions:

- Who are you?
- Where do I go?
- What documents do I need?
- What services are available?
- Can I scan a QR code?
- Can I speak Arabic, French, or English?

## First Sales Assets

- One-page PDF.
- 2-minute demo video.
- Pricing sheet.
- Pilot proposal template.
- Installation checklist.
- Privacy and safety summary.

## Validation Targets

Within 90 days:

- 10 customer discovery calls.
- 3 live demos.
- 1 pilot agreement.
- 1 real deployment, even if temporary.
