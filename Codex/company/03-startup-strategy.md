# Startup Strategy

## Strategic Positioning

The company should be positioned as:

> AI digital employees for Algerian and MENA organizations.

The first product is a live avatar kiosk, but the real platform is a multilingual knowledge, workflow, and video automation system.

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Local-First Experience

Support Arabic, French, and English from day one. Add Darja and Tamazight as differentiators when enough customer data and language testing are available.

### 2. Reusable Answers

Do not generate expensive avatar video for every repeated question. Use an Approved Answer Library:

- Match common questions semantically.
- Play cached videos instantly.
- Generate live answers only when needed.
- Save new Q&A as candidates for review.
- Approve and cache useful answers for future users.

### 3. Human Trust Layer

For sensitive domains, the system should answer only from approved content. If confidence is low, it should clarify, display a fallback, or hand off to a human.

### 4. Integration Over Model Ownership

At the start, use external APIs for:

- Avatar rendering.
- Speech-to-text.
- Text-to-speech.
- LLM reasoning.
- Translation.

Own:

- Product workflow.
- Customer data model.
- Admin dashboard.
- Knowledge base.
- Caching layer.
- Hardware deployment playbook.
- Local support.

### 5. Demo First, Platform Second

Win early customers with a physical demo that feels real. Then harden the platform behind it.

### 6. Build Bottom-Up

The product should climb the avatar quality ladder:

```text
Lite First
  ↓
Adaptive Hybrid
  ↓
Budgeted Premium
  ↓
Full Live Premium
```

Start with the cheapest useful assistant, then add premium realism, budget control, and enterprise depth as customer demand and revenue increase.

This lets the company validate the core problem before depending on expensive live avatar APIs.

## Initial Beachhead

Best first beachhead:

- AlgeriaTech event booth or media demo.
- Mall information assistant.
- Corporate reception assistant.

Avoid starting with government as the first customer. It may be a huge market later, but procurement and risk are too slow for the first validation cycle.

## Differentiation

Global avatar tools are strong at video generation. The company should be stronger at:

- Physical deployment.
- Local language workflows.
- Local content approval.
- Reusable video answer libraries.
- Customer-controlled avatar quality and budget policies.
- Business integrations.
- Support and maintenance.
- Regional sales relationships.
