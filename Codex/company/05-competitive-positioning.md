# Competitive Positioning

## Reference Companies

### HeyGen

Strengths:

- Fast avatar video generation.
- Live avatar and digital twin direction.
- Voice cloning and translation workflows.

What to learn:

- Make the avatar feel easy and magical.
- Reduce friction from script to final video.
- Support fast localization.

### Synthesia

Strengths:

- Enterprise polish.
- Brand control.
- Team workflows.
- Security and compliance positioning.

What to learn:

- Buyers need trust, governance, and consistency.
- Admin controls matter for serious customers.
- The product should look professional, not experimental.

### Colossyan

Strengths:

- Training video orientation.
- PPT/PDF-to-video workflows.
- Quizzes and learning use cases.

What to learn:

- AI video is more valuable when connected to business training and education.
- Turn documents into guided experiences.

### Easygenerator

Strengths:

- Course authoring.
- SCORM/LMS-oriented workflow.
- Learner management.

What to learn:

- Enterprise learning teams need structured content, not only videos.
- Assessments, exports, and tracking can increase contract value.

## Our Position

The startup should not compete head-to-head as a global AI video editor.

The stronger position is:

> The AI digital employee and multilingual service assistant platform for Algeria and MENA.
