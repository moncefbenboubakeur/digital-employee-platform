# Market Thesis

## Core Belief

The first strong opportunity is not generic AI video generation. It is AI digital employees for physical locations in Algeria and MENA.

Physical locations have repeated questions, multilingual visitors, long wait times, and a clear business reason to improve service quality. This creates a stronger B2B market than selling generic AI videos to individuals.

## Target Segments

### First Targets

- Malls and commercial centers.
- Corporate receptions.
- Technology events and trade shows.
- Universities and private schools.
- Coworking spaces and business centers.

### Later Targets

- Banks and insurance companies.
- Telecom branches.
- Hospitals and clinics.
- Hotels and tourism offices.
- Government offices.
- Airports and transport hubs.

## Pain Points

- Reception teams repeat the same answers all day.
- Visitors do not know where to go.
- Information changes but signs and printed material become outdated.
- Arabic/French/English support is inconsistent.
- Public offices have long queues and unclear procedures.
- Companies need better visitor experiences without hiring more staff.

## Why Now

- Real-time speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and LLMs are good enough for useful assistants.
- Avatar APIs make the demo visually convincing without training models from zero.
- Businesses already understand kiosks, QR codes, and digital signage.
- AI hype helps with sales conversations, but the product must solve a real operational problem.

## Startup Wedge

Start with a focused AI kiosk that answers common questions and guides visitors.

Then expand into:

- Custom knowledge bases.
- Appointment and queue integrations.
- Human handoff.
- Analytics.
- AI presenter video generation.
- Training and course content.
