# Company Vision

## One Sentence

Build the leading AI digital employee platform for Algeria and MENA, helping physical locations and media teams communicate through multilingual talking avatars, trusted answer libraries, and automated video workflows.

## The Bigger Idea

Many organizations in Algeria still depend on physical reception desks, printed signs, repeated explanations, and overloaded staff. Visitors ask the same questions every day:

- Where do I go?
- What documents do I need?
- What services are available?
- Can I scan a QR code?
- Can I speak Arabic, French, or English?

The product turns those repeated interactions into a digital assistant that can speak, guide, answer, display documents, show QR codes, and hand off to a human when needed.

## Long Term Vision

The long term company can become a platform for:

- AI receptionists in malls, banks, hotels, universities, hospitals, offices, and government buildings.
- AI public service assistants that explain procedures in multiple languages.
- AI sales and support avatars for Algerian businesses.
- AI presenter videos for media, training, onboarding, and internal communication.
- Course generation and learning modules for enterprise training.

## Local Advantage

The company should win by being local-first:

- Arabic and French bilingual workflows.
- Algerian Darja support over time.
- Local names, pronunciation, documents, places, and cultural context.
- On-site setup, hardware, and maintenance.
- Local billing, invoices, and enterprise procurement support.
- Ability to deploy private or on-premise systems for sensitive customers.

## What We Are Not

This is not a generic clone of HeyGen, Synthesia, Colossyan, or Easygenerator.

The goal is to combine the best ideas from those companies into a focused local platform:

- Real-time avatar conversation.
- Reusable video answers.
- Knowledge base and approved answers.
- Training and presentation video generation.
- Enterprise workflow and governance.
