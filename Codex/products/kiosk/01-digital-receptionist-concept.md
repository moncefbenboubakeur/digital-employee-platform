# Product Concept

## Working Product Name

AI Digital Receptionist.

Alternative names:

- Atlas Avatar
- Dziria Digital Assistant
- MENA Digital Employee
- AlgeriaTech AI Presenter Studio
- Smart Reception AI

## Product Summary

A multilingual AI avatar that can be placed in physical locations to answer common questions, guide visitors, show QR codes, and escalate to humans.

The product combines:

- Live conversational AI.
- Cached avatar videos for common answers.
- Admin-controlled knowledge base.
- Multilingual speech and subtitles.
- Analytics and review workflow.

## Main User Types

### Visitor

The person standing in front of the kiosk.

They want quick answers:

- Where do I go?
- What documents do I need?
- Who can help me?
- Can I scan a QR code?
- Which language can I use?

### Organization Admin

The customer staff member who manages answers.

They need to:

- Add approved answers.
- Review unknown questions.
- Update location information.
- See analytics.
- Control what the avatar is allowed to say.

### Operator

The person or team running deployment and maintenance.

They need to:

- Monitor kiosk health.
- Review errors.
- Manage customer settings.
- Control API cost.
- Deploy updates.

## Product Modes

### Instant Mode

The kiosk finds a high-confidence approved answer and plays the saved video immediately.

### Live Mode

The kiosk generates a live response for a new, non-sensitive question. The answer may use cheaper audio/TTS first instead of expensive avatar video.

### Safe Fallback Mode

For sensitive or low-confidence questions, the kiosk gives a safe response and routes the user to a human, QR code, or official page.

### Admin Review Mode

New questions and useful live answers are saved as candidates for later approval and caching.

## Avatar Quality Policy

The avatar mode should be configurable per customer, location, and budget.

Some customers may choose full live premium avatar responses for every safe answer. Others may choose an adaptive policy where cached approved answers use premium video, while new questions use a lightweight animated avatar and are upgraded later if useful.

Policy options:

- Full Live Premium.
- Budgeted Premium.
- Adaptive Hybrid.
- Lite First.

This keeps the product useful for both large enterprise customers and smaller budget-sensitive locations.
