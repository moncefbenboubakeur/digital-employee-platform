# YouTube Production Plan

## Starting Use Case

Use AlgeriaTech articles as the first production pipeline.

The goal is to publish consistent technology videos without building a large video team.

## Video Types

### Daily Brief

Length:

- 2 to 4 minutes.

Use:

- Quick coverage of one article or news item.

### Weekly Roundup

Length:

- 5 to 10 minutes.

Use:

- Summarize multiple AlgeriaTech articles.

### Explainer

Length:

- 6 to 12 minutes.

Use:

- Explain a technology, policy, startup, market, or product.

### Short

Length:

- 30 to 60 seconds.

Use:

- TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels.

## Production Pipeline

1. Select article.
2. Generate script.
3. Review script.
4. Generate scene plan.
5. Choose presenter/avatar style.
6. Generate or assign voice.
7. Add subtitles.
8. Render 16:9 and 9:16 versions.
9. Review final video.
10. Publish or schedule.

## Output Package

Each video should produce:

- Final video.
- Short vertical clip.
- SRT subtitles.
- YouTube title.
- YouTube description.
- Hashtags.
- Thumbnail headline.
- Social post text.

## Cost Control

Use the same caching logic where possible:

- Reuse intros.
- Reuse outros.
- Reuse presenter assets.
- Reuse common explainers.
- Use templates for repeated formats.

For early versions, external avatar APIs can be used. Later, the renderer can shift more work to Remotion/FFmpeg templates to reduce cost.
