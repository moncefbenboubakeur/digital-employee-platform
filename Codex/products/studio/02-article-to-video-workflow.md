# Article To Video Workflow

## Goal

Turn one article into a complete video package with minimal manual editing.

## Workflow

```text
Article URL or text
  ↓
Content extraction
  ↓
Fact summary
  ↓
Script generation
  ↓
Scene breakdown
  ↓
Presenter narration
  ↓
B-roll and visual suggestions
  ↓
Subtitle generation
  ↓
Video render
  ↓
YouTube package
```

## Step Details

### 1. Content Extraction

Input types:

- URL.
- Markdown.
- Plain text.
- PDF.
- Existing script.

Output:

- Clean article body.
- Headline.
- Author/source metadata.
- Key facts.
- Links and citations.

### 2. Script Generation

Script modes:

- News brief.
- Explainer.
- Business analysis.
- Educational.
- Short-form summary.

Each script should include:

- Hook.
- Main points.
- Transitions.
- Closing line.
- Optional call to action.

### 3. Scene Breakdown

Each scene includes:

- Presenter text.
- Visual idea.
- On-screen title.
- B-roll/image prompt.
- Duration estimate.
- Subtitle segment.

### 4. Editorial Review

The human editor can:

- Rewrite script lines.
- Remove weak claims.
- Add source notes.
- Change tone.
- Select video format.
- Approve final render.

### 5. Export

Export formats:

- Full YouTube video.
- YouTube Shorts.
- Subtitles as SRT/VTT.
- Thumbnail text ideas.
- Title and description.
- Social captions.

## Quality Rules

- Do not invent facts not present in the source.
- Keep source traceability for claims.
- Avoid overclaiming in headlines.
- Let editors approve before publishing.
- Preserve names, numbers, and dates carefully.
