# AI Presenter Studio Concept

## Product Summary

AI Presenter Studio turns articles, documents, scripts, and company updates into polished presenter-led videos.

The first internal use case is AlgeriaTech:

> AlgeriaTech article -> script -> scenes -> presenter narration -> subtitles -> YouTube video -> Shorts.

## Why This Product Exists

Many media teams and companies want video content, but video production is slow:

- Writing scripts takes time.
- Recording presenters is hard to schedule.
- Editing is expensive.
- Subtitles and translations are repetitive.
- Turning one article into multiple formats is manual.

The studio product makes that workflow faster and repeatable.

## Target Users

- AlgeriaTech editorial team.
- Local media publishers.
- Marketing teams.
- Company communication teams.
- Training teams that need explainer videos.
- Agencies producing content for clients.

## Core Jobs

### Article To Video

Paste a URL or article text and generate:

- Video script.
- Scene plan.
- Presenter narration.
- B-roll suggestions.
- Subtitles.
- YouTube title, description, and hashtags.

### Document To Video

Upload a PDF, report, or press release and generate:

- Explainer video.
- Executive summary.
- Slide-like scenes.
- Voiceover.
- Captions.

### Multi-Format Repurposing

From one source, export:

- 16:9 YouTube video.
- 9:16 Shorts/Reels/TikTok.
- 1:1 social clip.
- Text summary.
- Newsletter snippet.

## Product Differentiation

This product should not compete only on avatar quality. It should compete on workflow:

- Arabic/French/English content generation.
- Algeria/MENA editorial tone.
- Local pronunciation and names.
- YouTube-ready packaging.
- Reusable templates.
- Human editorial review.
- Integration with the kiosk and learning product lines.

## Relationship To Kiosk Product

The studio and kiosk share the same platform:

- Avatars.
- Voices.
- Translation.
- Subtitles.
- Media storage.
- Approved content.
- Analytics.

The difference is interaction mode:

- Kiosk is real-time, visitor-facing, and answer-driven.
- Studio is asynchronous, editor-facing, and content-driven.
