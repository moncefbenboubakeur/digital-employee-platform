# Product Portfolio

The company has three connected product lines.

## 1. AI Digital Receptionist

Primary wedge.

Use cases:

- Malls.
- Corporate receptions.
- Events.
- Universities.
- Public-service desks.

Why first:

- Clear B2B buyer.
- Strong physical demo.
- Repeated questions create obvious ROI.
- Local deployment and language support are real differentiators.

Build order:

```text
Lite First -> Adaptive Hybrid -> Budgeted Premium -> Full Live Premium
```

This lets the product start with a low-cost useful assistant and climb toward enterprise premium deployments.

## 2. AI Presenter Studio

Content creation product.

Use cases:

- AlgeriaTech article-to-video.
- YouTube explainers.
- Company announcements.
- Social clips.
- Press release videos.

Why second:

- Useful internally for AlgeriaTech.
- Creates demos and marketing assets.
- Shares avatar, voice, subtitle, and media infrastructure with the kiosk.

## 3. AI Learning

Enterprise training expansion.

Use cases:

- HR onboarding.
- Compliance training.
- Customer education.
- Internal SOP training.
- School and university modules.

Why later:

- Higher contract value.
- Needs more workflow depth.
- Requires assessment, progress, and LMS/export features.

## Shared Platform

All product lines share:

- Avatars.
- Voices.
- Subtitles.
- Translation.
- LLM orchestration.
- Knowledge bases.
- Media storage.
- Approval workflows.
- Analytics.

This prevents the company from becoming three separate products too early.
