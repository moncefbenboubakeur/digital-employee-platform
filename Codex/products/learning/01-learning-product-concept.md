# AI Learning Product Concept

## Product Summary

AI Learning turns company knowledge, PDFs, SOPs, policies, and presentations into training modules with presenter videos, quizzes, and learner-friendly summaries.

This product line is inspired by the training strengths of Colossyan and Easygenerator, but adapted for Algerian and MENA organizations.

## Why This Product Exists

Companies need to train employees, customers, and partners, but training material is often:

- Outdated.
- Too long.
- Not localized.
- Not interactive.
- Hard to track.
- Expensive to produce as video.

The learning product converts existing material into structured learning experiences.

## Target Users

- HR departments.
- Training teams.
- Compliance teams.
- Customer education teams.
- Schools and universities.
- Agencies building training content for clients.

## Core Jobs

### Document To Course

Input:

- PDF.
- Word document.
- PowerPoint.
- Internal policy.
- FAQ.

Output:

- Module outline.
- Lessons.
- Presenter videos.
- Quizzes.
- Summary notes.
- Glossary.

### Policy To Microlearning

Turn HR, safety, or compliance policies into:

- Short lessons.
- Scenario questions.
- Knowledge checks.
- Completion certificate later.

### Training Video Generator

Turn one lesson into:

- Presenter-led explainer.
- Subtitles.
- Quiz.
- Downloadable summary.

## Relationship To Other Product Lines

The learning product shares:

- AI presenter generation from Studio.
- Approved knowledge and safety rules from Kiosk.
- Translation, voices, subtitles, and media storage from Platform.

The learning product differs because it needs:

- Course structure.
- Assessments.
- Learner progress.
- SCORM/xAPI or LMS export later.
