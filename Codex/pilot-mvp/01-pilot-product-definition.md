# Pilot Product Definition

## Product Name

Working name: **Digital Receptionist Lite**

## One Sentence

A multilingual kiosk assistant that answers common visitor questions with cached approved answers, simple voice/subtitle output, and a lightweight animated avatar.

## Target Customer For Pilot

The first pilot should target a location with repeated visitor questions but low technical complexity.

Best first customer profile:

- one physical location
- 20 to 300 visitors per day
- repeated questions
- staff currently answers the same questions all day
- service information can be written as FAQs
- no sensitive account-specific operations in MVP

Good examples:

- mall reception desk
- small public-service office
- municipal desk
- post-office-like branch
- private clinic reception
- company lobby

Avoid in first pilot:

- bank transactions
- medical diagnosis
- legal decisions
- identity verification
- payments
- complex personal records

## First User Problems

Visitors ask:

- Who are you?
- Where do I go?
- What services are available?
- What documents do I need?
- What are the opening hours?
- Can I scan a QR code?
- Can I speak Arabic/French/English?
- Who should I talk to?
- What should I do if I do not understand?

Staff problem:

- repeated interruptions
- same explanations all day
- multilingual friction
- visitors waiting in the wrong line
- paper instructions hard to update

Customer problem:

- wants better visitor experience
- wants low monthly cost
- wants no surprise invoice
- wants answers controlled by admin
- wants simple setup

## MVP Output

The kiosk should respond with:

- visible answer text
- spoken audio
- subtitles
- simple 2D/lite avatar movement
- QR code or direction when useful
- human escalation when uncertain

## What Is In Scope

- Arabic/French/English language switch
- typed input first
- voice input as optional phase two
- approved answer cache
- unknown question capture
- safe fallback answer
- admin dashboard for questions and answers
- simple location/services/documents knowledge
- usage analytics
- daily cost tracking

## What Is Out Of Scope For First Pilot

- full premium live avatar
- identity verification
- payments
- private account lookup
- multi-branch enterprise rollout
- custom avatar training
- full document ingestion automation
- complex appointment scheduling
- integration with government backend systems

## Pilot Success Definition

The pilot is successful if:

- at least 60 percent of visitor questions are answered from cache after setup
- staff confirms repeated questions decreased
- visitors can use Arabic/French without staff help
- unknown questions become a useful improvement queue
- average response time feels instant for cached answers
- cost remains predictable
- customer asks to keep or expand the system
